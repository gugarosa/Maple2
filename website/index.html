The MS2 installer is on hold for antivirus review. No game-client download is published here.

Client requirements

Windows 10 or newer, x64 (not ARM), and the compatible NA client 20.8.1.0316. Mushroom is the launcher, not the game-client download. Keep your original game files.

Manual connection for invited testers
  1. In Mushroom, choose the client root containing Data and x64, not the x64 folder itself.
  2. Add MapleTime MS2 Azure with Login host 20.226.79.46 and port 20001.
  3. Leave automatic login off. Select that server, launch the game, and enter the account you registered.
