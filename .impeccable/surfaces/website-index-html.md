# Surface brief: Homepage

Scope: `website/index.html` and its portal layout in `website/styles.css`.
Visitor mode: **Persuade** at arrival, **Operate** in setup instructions,
**Read** in project updates and pilot-status prose.

The approved direction below is retained. Its account-to-client story describes
the journey, not account-page composition. Registration/notices and missing-page
recovery have their own matching briefs. Shared tokens belong to root
[DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md), not to this surface's target list.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar MapleTime companion opening onto a different, block-built
world. Keep one account-to-client-to-connection path; remove competing card
directories rather than adding another layer of navigation.

OWN-WORLD: MS1-family maple-orange actions, neutral light surfaces and a dark
tonal ladder, readable editorial heading hierarchy, restrained rules, and
compact rounded controls. The original cyan cube and authentic MS2 panoramic
artwork supply the second game's identity. No MS1 sprites or claims.

STORY: Recognize MS2, understand the private-pilot limit, register a separate
account, prepare the existing client, and find exact Mushroom connection steps.
Success and failure must say only what the server knows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Open navigation, a generous two-column title/action composition,
and an unmasked full-width MS2 panorama. The main start action and pilot
restriction precede the image, including on mobile. A compact game logo names
the artwork. Below, an ordered unboxed setup sequence leads into readable
requirements, native connection disclosure, real updates, and honest status.
Signature interaction: the native connection disclosure puts the complete
connection recipe within reach; focus, target and open state are visible.
Motion is limited to short control feedback, removed under reduced motion.

FORM: User-confirmed existing-world refinement, following the referenced MS1
process and MS2's approved assets. No replacement-world seed, catalogue card,
image-generation comp, or comp-led build is claimed. Code-led, without image
generation; family resemblance is pinned rather than subject to a concept roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: retain all public anchors, the approved-network registration link,
source attribution, original artwork bytes, strict static CSP, no scripts/fonts
or credential collection on the portal, and all backend registration safeguards.
No new product facts, game-server features, public launch, or downloadable client.

Family translation: preserve MS1's neutral/orange hierarchy and dark tonal
ladder, with Georgia and system UI fonts as local, zero-download equivalents.
Keep MS2's cube, class panorama and creature imagery. MS1's larger navigation,
live status, wiki, account-recovery backend and downloadable installer are not
capabilities of this pilot and must not be imitated.

## Built basis and handoff

The implementation in `website/index.html` and `website/styles.css` retains the
contract's open navigation, artwork-first identity and ordered setup. Its status
container is factual prose, not a fabricated health/metric display.
The bounded finish review returned `ship`; root DESIGN.md and the schema-v2
sidecar discharge documentation, not deployment. Artwork provenance remains in
`deploy/azure/README.md#website-artwork` and the pinned raster manifest, never
new image metadata. No seed, comp or new per-route human approval was created
by this post-build scope split.
